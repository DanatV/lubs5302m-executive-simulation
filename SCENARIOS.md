# Scenarios — version 2.0.0

All figures are authored fictional teaching outcomes, independent of random forest predictions. Every team receives the same external development; no random team-based outcome differences exist. Conversion is annual intake divided by 8,000. Experience is a game index out of 100. Rankings are illustrative relative positions, not purchased or guaranteed returns. No overall score is calculated.

## Baseline

Rank 96; 2,000 annual enrolments; 25% conversion; experience 65. Annual standard tuition £24,000. Ordinary operating costs are excluded.

## Exact first-round portfolios

Only these complete portfolios are permitted. Subsets or other combinations are not allowed, even if affordable. Full scholarships £2m; half scholarships £1m. Full staff programme £3m; limited programme £2m.

## Common external development

The first recruitment cycle has closed. Competitors have increased their financial support, and the next recruitment market is becoming more price-sensitive. A forthcoming ranking update also puts pressure on universities near the top-100 boundary. The board has released a final £500,000 for one targeted adjustment.

This is fictional competitive pressure, not a real ranking-methodology announcement. Historical models are not retrained.

## Teaching trade-offs

A protects reputation; C emphasises delivery experience; D grows intake most but can strain capacity. These relationships are authored for discussion rather than inferred treatment effects. The relative rank decline outside A is a scenario assumption. Students should consider controllability, additional return, time to implement, capacity, affordability and evidence uncertainty. No demographic access metric or profit measure is invented.

Version 2 interim ranks and D intake were revised by the lecturer. Final numerical outcomes remain unchanged; D final intake is below its revised first-cycle intake.
